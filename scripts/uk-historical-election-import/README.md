# UK national parliamentary chronology

Checked 9 October 2026. The fixture contains 101 elections: all 58 UK House of Commons general elections, 1802–2024; 41 general selections of the sixteen Scottish representative peers, 1802–1959; and the two 1999 retained hereditary-peer ballots (15 office-holders and 75 party/group seats). The UK's upper chamber is the House of Lords, not a Senate. These historic elected components do not represent elections of the whole upper chamber.

The five existing official Commons records for 2010–2024 are preserved exactly from `pre-import-backup.json`, including identifiers, party references, ideology metadata and version 3. There are 96 new records. Parliamentary predecessors before the 1801 union, individual vacancy by-elections, devolved elections, referendums, European elections and appointments are outside this import. The initial Irish representative-peer selection occurred before the UK union; subsequent Irish selections filled individual vacancies. No future national polling date is inferred from a maximum parliamentary term.

## Evidence and gaps

Commons Library dates take precedence from 1832 onward. Polling intervals are retained; 1945 ends with the delayed constituency polls on 19 July, not the 26 July results announcement. Commons Library seat capacities control the chronology. Primary Library XLSX national results supply 1918–2005 additions. Published historical categories are retained explicitly: Conservative allies, Coalition/National/Independent Liberal categories, and the SNP/Plaid Cymru aggregate are not falsely presented as individual-party counts. Explicit Northern Ireland parties from 1974 are deducted once from the UK Other category and their shares use the UK vote denominator. Historical source-rounded percentages may differ from shares recalculated from exact published vote totals. Turnout follows the Library's contested-electorate conventions. Multi-member and plural voting are marked with `weightedVotes`; candidate selections are not claimed to be unique voters.

Earlier Commons results follow reviewed historical national tables and bibliographies. The first nine contests show leading factions with partial coverage. The 1806 secondary allocation exceeds the authoritative capacity, so conflicting seat figures are withheld. The 1847 secondary allocation also fails the authoritative capacity check and is marked partial. Missing allocations are never filled by arithmetic or guessed party affiliations.

Scottish general dates use the historical chronology and bibliography, with the National Records of Scotland archive identified. Hansard confirms the final 6 October 1959 contest. Its 29 candidate endorsement totals and sixteen elected members are transcribed, without invented percentages or turnout. Other Scottish contests currently have dates and component capacities but no transcribed candidate results; they remain partial and not entered. Peers physically present are not the full electorate. Although a secondary list labels 13 January 1922 as general, primary Hansard on 7 February 1922 confirms it filled only three vacancies; it is excluded.

The two 1999 winning rosters use Parliament's official publications. The 2013 Companion, section 1.04 footnote 5, confirms actual polling on 27–28 October and 3–4 November, distinct from announcement dates. The 75 seats have preassigned quotas of Conservative 42, Crossbench 28, Liberal Democrat 3 and Labour 2; these are not public vote shares. Neither contest has invented raw vote totals or denominators, so coverage remains partial despite complete winning rosters. The two office-holders retained without election are excluded. Hereditary membership rights ended under the 2026 reform; no future Lords general election is invented.

## Reproduction and validation

`election-links.json` and `primary-links.json` identify downloads. Cache HTML under `.cache/uk-history/<key>.html` and XLSX/CSV under their matching extensions. Run `python scripts/extract-uk-historical-primary.py`, then `python scripts/import-uk-historical-elections.py` with BeautifulSoup and the repository's table helper available. The XLSX reader uses ZIP/XML directly and does not modify source spreadsheets. `source-audit.json` records hashes of downloaded evidence; `primary-extract.json` stores primary control values and source definitions. `reconciliation.json` summarizes all 96 additions. The long constituency CSV is archived as supporting evidence; published national counts come from the Library's national XLSX, avoiding unopposed sentinel values in the constituency CSV.

Run `node scripts/check-uk-historical-elections.mjs`, `pnpm test`, `pnpm typecheck`, and a fresh `pnpm build`. The historical checker verifies chronology counts, exact baseline preservation, model validation, polling intervals, official national totals, peer quotas, reciprocal related-election links, and absence of the misclassified 1922 vacancy election. Publication stages only the 96 additions privately before guarded updates publish their reviewed payloads. No schema or authorization changes are needed.
