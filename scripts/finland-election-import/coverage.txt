National Eduskunta elections from 1907 through 2023 and presidential elections from 1919 through 2024, including popular rounds, public elector elections and the final decisive parliamentary or electoral-college ballot. These different ballot types are recorded separately. The 1944 appointment by special law and the 1973 term extension were not contested presidential elections. Finland has a unicameral parliament. Parliamentary turnout from 1908 uses Statistics Finland’s resident-citizen series; it excludes eligible citizens living abroad. Earlier estate-based Diet elections are outside this coverage.
