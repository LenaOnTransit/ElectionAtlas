# Poland national general-election archive, 1919–2025

This import uses the existing `WorldElection` and `CountryParty` models: 27 Sejm elections, 16 Senate elections and 22 presidential election/round records. It also records the unheld 10 May 2020 presidential vote explicitly as cancelled and the confirmed 22 November 2026 Senate district 20 by-election for the Calendar. European Parliament, local elections, referendums and a comprehensive historical Senate by-election series are outside this general-election import. No precise date is invented for a future 2027 general election.

`records.json` and `parties.json` are reviewable import data. `reconciliation.json` preserves each table's reported vote denominator and seat total. `primary-reconciliation.json` checks every 2025 presidential candidate against the certified PKW announcements. The 2023 Sejm votes reconcile with all 41 official district exports. Earlier post-transition results are cross-checked with PKW's historical calendar. Older figures are transcribed from the cited historical result tables and their Nohlen & Stöver bibliography; they are not all newly checked against original individual protocols. `source-audit.json` records fetched source hashes and known unavailable or incomplete exports.

The table extractor retains source-cell identities, preventing repeated coalition rowspan votes from being counted for each constituent party. First rounds and runoffs have separate records and denominators. Decisive assembly ballots are marked indirect; no popular vote is inferred from presidential votes in 1922–1989. Piłsudski's successful but declined 31 May 1926 election is distinct from Mościcki's election on 1 June. Presidents in exile chosen by designation and the Communist-era Council of State chairmanship are not presented as popular presidential elections.

The 1989 Sejm has 299 reserved and 161 freely contested seats, while the restored 100-seat Senate was freely contested. Seat allocation is recorded after both rounds; incomparable national-list, constituency, candidate-mark and round votes are not combined. Pre-2011 Senate percentages measure multiple candidate-vote marks rather than shares of voters. The 1935 and 1938 Senate records describe the full 96-member chamber, including 32 presidential appointees and 64 indirectly selected senators. Single-front Communist-era results are labelled noncompetitive declared figures, not genuine competitive party preferences; shared front votes are not invented for constituent parties.

Four historical tables intentionally retain partial coverage: 1919 has changing territorial/co-option and seat scopes; the 1922 Sejm rows omit 11,833 votes compared with the source denominator; the 1935 Sejm table accounts for 206 of 208 places; the 1957 table accounts for 459 of 460. Gaps are not assigned to invented parties or residual votes. Earlier national boundaries and franchise rules are described in each record.

Profiles preserve election-era list/committee names, with historical entities distinguished from similarly named modern groups. Colours are an editorial palette, not verified official branding. No ideology is inferred. Profile links describe recorded support/list affiliation, not proof of a candidate's legal membership. No country-level legitimacy assessment or other country's election record is overwritten.

Reproduce with cached snapshots under `.cache/poland`, `source-tables.json`, `links.json`, and the official 2023 CSV/2025 certified announcement inputs: `python scripts/import-polish-elections.py`; then `node scripts/check-polish-elections.mjs`. Database application is separate: stage new records as drafts at version 1, validate, and publish only the verified IDs at version 2. Existing rows must never be overwritten by this new-country import.
