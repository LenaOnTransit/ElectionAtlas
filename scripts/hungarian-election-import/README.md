# Hungary national archive

Uses the existing WorldElection/CountryParty schema and publication workflow. No database schema, country assessment, historical classification or moderator permissions are changed.

Scope: 68 national election/ballot records from the Reform Age in 1825 through 2026: 50 parliamentary events and 18 presidential selections/assembly ballots. Includes historical representative-chamber elections, the estates-based Diet, the staggered 1920–1921 territorial elections, the 1944–1945 provisional assembly, Communist-era elections, all post-1990 general elections, individual assembly presidential ballots, and the 2026 parliamentary and presidential elections. Hereditary/appointed upper houses, regents/monarchs and chairs of the collective Presidential Council are not reclassified as general parliamentary or individual presidential elections. Local elections, European Parliament ballots, referendums and a comprehensive constituency by-election archive are outside this national general-election import. No confirmed future national polling date through 31 December 2027 was established at the 13 October 2026 review; no Calendar date is invented.

## Sources and reproduction

`links.json` identifies secondary result tables and their bibliographies. `source-tables.json` contains factual cells with shared identities so rowspan coalition votes are not duplicated. `source-audit.json` stores retrieved page hashes and explicit retrieval failures. Older tables cite historical handbooks and contemporary records; most modern tables cite NVI. Not every secondary transcription was independently checked against its original protocol.

The entire 2026 national-list vote distribution (five party lists and twelve minority lists, 6,366,733 valid ballots) was checked against NVI’s official legacy national protocol. Its major-party list counts and final 199-member composition are recorded in `primary-reconciliation.json`: Tisza 141, Fidesz–KDNP 52, Our Homeland 6. The final list participation denominator is 6,408,362 participating voters out of 8,112,646 registered, distinct from the 6,404,403 counted ballot papers. The official government announcement of 11 August 2026 verifies András Baka’s indirect presidential selection with 140 votes for and 6 against; entry into office on 19 August is not mistaken for the election date. Hungary’s National Archives and National Remembrance Committee support the 1946 and 1948 presidential selections. Public-broadcaster historical chronology supports the provisional 1919 council selection. NVI’s modern JavaScript site and Parliament pages sometimes require rendering/CAPTCHA; the public NVI legacy protocol supplied independently retrievable numerical results.

To reproduce: retrieve each successful `links.json` URL to `.cache/hungary/<key>.html`, then run:

```sh
PYTHONPATH=/path/to/beautifulsoup python scripts/extract-hungarian-election-sources.py
python scripts/import-hungarian-elections.py
node scripts/check-hungarian-elections.mjs
pnpm test
pnpm build
```

The generator never writes to the database. The pre-import snapshot contains the existing Hungary country record; there were no Hungarian elections or party profiles. Draft insertion, exact stored-payload verification and version-guarded publication are separate steps. The already paginated shared party catalogue supports the additional profiles beyond the API’s 1,000-row limit.

## Methodology and limitations

- Modern displayed vote shares use proportional/list ballots only. Constituency votes, later constituency rounds, compensation/fractional votes and list ballots are not added together. The seat allocation covers the entire historical chamber: 386 mandates through 2010, 199 from 2014. National-minority voting members are included when elected; non-voting minority spokespeople are excluded.
- For two-round general elections, one record carries the election-cycle dates and final whole-chamber allocation. Its list denominator does not combine first- and second-round constituency totals. Presidential assembly ballots have individual records and denominators. In 2000 and 2005, a majority of valid votes in an early ballot did not satisfy the two-thirds-of-all-members rule; winners are marked only in the decisive ballot. The 2017 first-ballot 131-vote result likewise did not elect Áder; the second ballot did.
- Presidential counts are assembly ballot outcomes, not popular presidential votes. Public turnout is null. Votes against a sole candidate are valid ballot outcomes, not a second party/candidate. Independent candidates remain independent even when nominated by a party alliance.
- Estates-based Diet group alignments and small representative-vote columns are not treated as a popular election. Only the source-scoped representative allocation is entered. Hereditary/appointed upper-chamber membership, Croatian/transitional representation and institutional seats outside a reported table are not assigned guessed party results. Historical source-scoped capacities may differ from full constitutional or later-session membership.
- The 1920 cycle has different territorial cohorts through October 1921; only its final 219-seat allocation is entered. The provisional 1944 assembly grew from an initial 230 to 498 representatives through later territorial selections; the final composition is explicitly labelled as an indirect selection.
- Single-front shared vote cells are counted once across constituent parties/independents. In 1985, different shared constituency and national-list vote denominators are omitted; the known membership allocation is 288 Socialist Workers’ Party members, 98 independents and one vacancy, together accounting for 387 seats. The vacancy is a neutral allocation row, not an invented party.
- Historical restricted suffrage, open/secret voting, uncontested mandates, the 1947 blue-ballot fraud and Communist front elections are described. Declared official totals are not a reconstruction of genuine voter preference. No modern ideology or country legitimacy assessment is copied into historical results.
- Two elections, 1830 and 1848, lack verified party/group result tables and are explicitly `not-entered`, partial. The 1919, 1946 and 1948 presidential selections have known selected candidates but unverified precise ballot counts; these remain null with partial numerical coverage. They require manual archival review. Date precision and sorting anchors are explicit where only month/year is established.
- Party/front/coalition labels retain source-era identities. Profiles have no inferred ideology; colours are an editorial display palette, not an official-branding claim. Historical organisations are distinguished from modern names. Generic independent/vacancy/against rows do not become artificial saved party profiles.

`reconciliation.json` provides per-record seat/vote audits. The checker validates every existing model field and tests modern capacities, mixed ballots, staggered elections, shared front cells, the legitimate vacancy, separate assembly ballots and their thresholds, independent candidates, saved party references, and all independently verified 2026 list counts. Existing protected integrity findings remain open for manual review; none is automatically accepted or used to alter results.

Import verification: all 68 stored draft payloads and all 378 party profiles matched the validated files exactly; the existing country record and assessment were unchanged. Only those version-guarded drafts were published at version 2. The full test suite and production build passed. The generated archive contains all 68 links and all 68 event/result pages, and none of the Hungarian records appears on Live Elections. The automatic integrity checker reported zero errors, two missing-table warnings and three informational final/partial findings for the early presidential selections. These five findings remain open for manual review.
