# Italian national election archive

86 records: 22 Kingdom Chamber elections (1861–1934), the 1946 Constituent Assembly, 19 Republic Chamber and 19 elected Senate elections (1948–2022), 10 European elections (1979–2024), 13 constitutional presidential elections and two provisional head-of-state elections. Chamber and Senate elections remain separate, linked records with separate electorates. Presidential results use the decisive indirect ballot, not popular votes or summed rounds.

The Presidency's official tables take precedence for presidential elections. The Senate's validated 2001 and 2022 tables and Parliament's final European 2024 shares take precedence over secondary summaries. The 2022 Senate has 189 main-table seats, seven regional seats and four overseas seats, totalling 200. Regional and foreign shares are not mixed into the main denominator. The official 2001 table assigns 314 of 315 seats; one seat remains unallocated here. No raw 2024 European vote counts are inferred from rounded primary percentages. European seats are the elected delegation: 72 in 2009 and 73 before Brexit in 2019.

Earlier historical results rely on reviewed secondary tables and their bibliographies. Incomplete or inconsistent totals remain partial. Conflicting 1994 Senate seat allocations are omitted rather than displaying an overfull diagram. Restricted historical franchise, Fascist approval votes and the separation of elected senators from life/by-right senators are explicit. The 1929 and 1934 single-list votes are marked uncompetitive. Party rankings are not government-formation claims.

Scope excludes pre-unification Sardinian elections, appointed chambers, local/regional contests, individual by-elections and referendums. The 1946 referendum is separate from the constituent election. There is no direct prime-minister election. No future polling day is invented from an expected parliamentary term.
