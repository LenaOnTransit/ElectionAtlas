National elections from 1950–2023: Grand National Assembly elections, Senate elections and renewals (1961–1979), direct presidential ballots (2014–2023), and decisive parliamentary presidential selections (1950–2007). Earlier elections, referendums, local elections and failed presidential processes are outside this series. Historical source discrepancies are marked partial and explained on the relevant pages. Senate seats count each renewal, while parliamentary seats follow ballot lists rather than later party membership.
