# Denmark national election archive

87 new historical records: all 71 Folketing elections from December 1849 through November 2022, and 16 documented Landsting constituency/electoral-college elections from 1898 to 1953. The existing 2026 record receives a country/year title, party references, colours and matched 2022 previous seats; its certified votes, shares and seats are unchanged. Denmark is a monarchy and has no popular presidential election series.

Every table is labelled partial. Mainland party counts are kept separate from Faroe Islands and Greenland results, preserving their own vote-share denominators. Early unopposed returns, restricted franchise, unavailable vote totals and changing district boundaries limit comparisons. Upper-house rows use seats won through constituency electors, excluding appointments, continuing seats and members chosen separately by the outgoing chamber. The upper-house vote column, where available, describes the public election of electors, not ballots cast by electors. Early Højre organisations and nineteenth-century Left factions are not silently merged into later parties. New Alliance retains its 2007 identity without inheriting the later Liberal Alliance ideology label.

Statistics Denmark's official 2019 report, table 43, independently reconciles all mainland seat allocations in 1971–2019. Its bibliography resolves the 1915 date as 7 May. Certified Statistics Denmark pages verify every mainland party vote in 2022 and the existing 2026 count. Earlier and upper-house allocations are transcribed from the linked historical tables and bibliographies; not every number is independently verified against an original return. `source-extract.json` preserves source hashes and expanded table cells. `reconciliation.json` records totals and the precise seat column used. Party histories are linked from saved profiles and attribution fields; unassessed labels stay unclassified.

The 1848 Constituent Assembly, whose party allocations are reported as ranges, and the separate 1865 Rigsrådets Folketing page, whose table requires further verification, are not published as exact chamber results. Earlier Landsting elections lacking verified allocations are not added as empty placeholders.

Reproduce with `PYTHONPATH=.cache/python-libs python scripts/extract-denmark-sources.py` after downloading the public URLs in `links.json` to `.cache/denmark/<page>.html`, then `python scripts/import-denmark-elections.py`. Source control files can be checked using `python scripts/reconcile-denmark-primary.py` with the downloaded official PDF converted by Poppler, and `node scripts/check-denmark-elections.mjs`. The build uses the committed extracts; downloads are not required for routine tests. `build-denmark-import-sql.py` creates an atomic, idempotent transaction that rejects changed baselines and conflicting IDs. `pre-import-backup.json` contains only existing public Denmark data. No schema or authorization changes.
