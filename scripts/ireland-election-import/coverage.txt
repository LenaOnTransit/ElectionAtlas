Dáil elections from the all-Ireland 1918 Westminster precursor through 2024, all presidential elections from 1938 through 2025, and Seanad elections from 1922 through 2025. Results distinguish first preferences, transferred presidential counts, elected senators and nominees. Unopposed returns have no invented votes or turnout. Some historical vote aggregates disagree with their component figures; those discrepancies are identified. Dates use year or month precision where an exact polling date is not established.
